Idea:
My idea is to build a fitness website which allows user to workout with other people remotely and interact with them.
The inspiration came from myself. I used to workout 3-4 times a week with my friends during my undergrad study. I am a very lazy person to be honest. However, this roomate of mine is really disciplined. 
He goes to the gym 3-4 times a week. When living with people like this, even lazy people like me start to go to gym and workout with him. 
When we work out together, he keeps me accountable and doesn't let me slack off.
After I came to Austrlia for postgraduate study, I never went to gym for even 1 time. Therefore, I am thinking if there is an app that allows user to work out with your friends remotely in a very interesting way.
Like the web can be a cartoon gym. Each user can have their own avatar. They should be able to sign up and log in with their email and password. After they log in, they should be able to start an group workout or join an existing group workout through passcode. The website should support all the functions that a normal fitness app would have like record the excercise, weight, rest time, etc... The core feature should be the avatar will do the same excercise in the simulated web gym when the user select the excercise they are doing. When user hit rest, their avatar should also take a break. Of course user should be able to interact with each other. When in the same group workout, user should be able to see other people's avatars who are also in the same group workout and whether they are taking a break or in excercise. User should be able to interact with other people's avatar like go to them and slap their face (not sure yet) or something and send certain message. That message can be just random shit or inspiring people to stop slacking off and go back to workout.

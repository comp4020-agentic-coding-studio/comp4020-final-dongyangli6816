State: error · not rendered on home

Home renders no errors of its own. An empty passcode is stopped by the field's required. A wrong, closed or full room posts to /join, which shows the error under its own slots, with the code kept (the join page's design). A ?left or ?ended value that isn't a well-formed passcode is ignored: no bubble.
